    How it works

    1. Training dataThousands of real and fake postings from the EMSCAD dataset (Kaggle).
    2. Text to numbersEach posting is cleaned and turned into up to 300 word IDs from a 20,000-word vocabulary.
    3. BiLSTM classifierA bidirectional LSTM reads the words in order, both ways, and scores how likely the posting is a scam.
    4. ExplanationLIME removes words at random hundreds of times to see which ones move the score. Red-flag rules add plain-language checks.
    5. Verdict and reasonsYou see the risk level, the marked-up posting, and the red flags behind it.

    About the project

    Online recruitment platforms are increasingly targeted by fake job postings and phishing scams. Existing deep learning detectors work well but act as black boxes. This project adds an explainability layer so every prediction comes with a human-readable justification.
