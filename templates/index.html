    What to do next

    • Don’t pay anything. No registration fee, deposit or “refundable” amount. Real employers don’t charge you to get a job.
    • Don’t share documents or codes. Keep your Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, UPI PIN and OTPs to yourself.
    • Check the company yourself. Find its official website or LinkedIn page and contact it from there, not through the number or email in the post.
    • Already paid or shared details? Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) straight away and report it at cybercrime.gov.in.

        How it works

        1. You paste the post Text, or a screenshot from WhatsApp or a job site.
        2. The AI reads it A model trained on thousands of real and fake job ads scores it.
        3. Red flags are checked Fees, ID or bank requests, fake company websites, WhatsApp-only contact and other tricks.
        4. It shows you why The words that mattered are highlighted, and a second model double-checks.
        5. You decide See the risk level and what to watch out for, before you share anything.
        Technical details
        Training data
        EMSCAD dataset (Kaggle: 17,880 postings, 866 fake) augmented with 1,200 India-specific examples (half scams, half genuine) covering patterns EMSCAD lacks.
        Preprocessing
        Lower-cased, punctuation removed, turned into up to 300 word IDs from a 20,000-word vocabulary.
        Model
        Bidirectional LSTM (served with TensorFlow Lite); flags a scam at 50% or more. Baseline: TF-IDF + Logistic Regression.
        Explanation
        LIME on the model's log-odds (500 samples); exact coefficient weights for the baseline.
        Red flags
        13 checks; each adds to the log-odds (+1.5 for the 7 serious ones: money, ID or bank details, passports, remote-access apps, task scams, fake government jobs and fake company websites; +0.5 for the others; max +4).
        Screenshots
        Read in your browser with Tesseract.js OCR; the image is never uploaded.

        About the project

        Online recruitment platforms are increasingly targeted by fake job postings and phishing scams. Existing deep learning detectors work well but act as black boxes. This project adds an explainability layer so every prediction comes with a human-readable justification.

        Meet Detective Owlbert, our owl mascot. He gives scam-spotting tips, reacts to every result and hosts the Spot the Scam game. No scam escapes him.
