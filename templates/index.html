    How it works

    1. You paste the post Text, or a screenshot from WhatsApp or a job site.
    2. The AI reads it A model trained on thousands of real and fake job ads scores it.
    3. Red flags are checked Fees, ID or bank requests, WhatsApp-only contact and other tricks.
    4. It shows you why The words that mattered are highlighted, and a second model double-checks.
    5. You decide See the risk level and what to watch out for, before you share anything.
    Technical details
    Training data
    Real and fake job postings from the EMSCAD dataset (Kaggle).
    Preprocessing
    Lower-cased, punctuation removed, turned into up to 300 word IDs from a 20,000-word vocabulary.
    Model
    Bidirectional LSTM (served with TensorFlow Lite); scam threshold 0.61. Baseline: TF-IDF + Logistic Regression.
    Explanation
    LIME on the model's log-odds (500 samples); exact coefficient weights for the baseline.
    Red flags
    8 rules; each adds to the log-odds (+1.5 for money or ID requests, +0.75 for others, max +4).
    Screenshots
    Read in your browser with Tesseract.js OCR; the image is never uploaded.

    About the project

    Online recruitment platforms are increasingly targeted by fake job postings and phishing scams. Existing deep learning detectors work well but act as black boxes. This project adds an explainability layer so every prediction comes with a human-readable justification.
